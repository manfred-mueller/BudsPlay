BudsPlay lets you control two audio files directly via the tap 
gestures of your Bluetooth earbuds – without ever touching 
your phone.

Simply select two audio files and you're ready to go:

- Double tap right → play Track 1
- Triple tap right → play Track 2

Your selected files are saved permanently and are ready 
instantly on next launch.

FEATURES
- Supports MP3, WAV, FLAC, OGG, AAC, M4A and more
- Progress bar with elapsed and remaining time display
- Manual scrubbing via the progress bar
- Compatible with all Bluetooth earbuds supporting AVRCP
- Runs in the background – control works on locked screen too
- No ads, no in-app purchases, no data collection

PRIVACY
BudsPlay only stores the file paths of your selected audio
files locally on your device. No personal data is collected
or shared.
